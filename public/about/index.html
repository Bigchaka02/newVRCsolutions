VRC About the business

Built on verifiable quality. Backed by independent third-party lab reports.

VRC exists because the research compound industry has too many anonymous shells, hidden lab reports, and quiet efficacy claims. We are the opposite of that.

Why we exist

Two patterns we wanted to break.

1. The anonymous LLC.

Most peptide vendors operate from offshore mailbox addresses, behind LLCs with no named officers, with operations hidden from customers. If something goes wrong with their product, there is no one to reach. If they shut down — and many do, frequently — accounts, COA records, and refund options vanish with them.

VRC operates under VRC Solutions LLC, a registered limited liability company. Our address is public. Our tax and banking registrations are on file with the state. Our customer-service channels are documented and consistent.

2. The hidden Certificate of Analysis.

Many vendors claim their products are “third-party tested” but ask customers to email for the COA, or hide it behind an account login, or only show one for a single batch from years ago. Worse, some publish COAs that don’t match the product.

Every batch we sell is tested by an independent third-party laboratory. Every COA is published in our public COA library and linked directly from the product page. Verifiable before purchase. No emails required. No account required.

What we do, what we don’t

Our principles, plainly stated.

What we do

  • Sell only laboratory research compounds and reference materials
  • Third-party test every batch through an independent laboratory
  • Publish every COA in our public library
  • Operate under a named US LLC with a public Kentucky address
  • Maintain strict Research Use Only discipline in all communications
  • Ship the next business day, tracked US shipping, discreet packaging
  • Refund orders that arrive damaged or are lost in transit
  • Treat compliance as a feature, not an inconvenience

What we don’t do

  • Sell semaglutide or tirzepatide (Novo Nordisk / Eli Lilly IP)
  • Sell SARMs, hCG, or controlled-substance-adjacent compounds
  • Provide dosing protocols, reconstitution instructions, or human-use guidance
  • Make any therapeutic, efficacy, or health claims
  • Refund based on research outcomes
  • Ship outside the United States
  • Operate behind an anonymous LLC or offshore shell
Our commitment

What we commit to, in writing.

  • Publishing the COA for every batch we sell, before we sell it
  • Maintaining a registered US LLC with a verifiable address
  • Treating customers as researchers, not as drug consumers
  • Maintaining a strict Research Use Only policy across the site, customer communications, and marketing
  • Never making claims about what our products do for the human or animal body
  • Refusing to sell products that put customers or the company at disproportionate risk

This commitment is not marketing. It is how we operate.
