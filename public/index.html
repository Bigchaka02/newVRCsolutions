Two patterns we wanted to break

The research compound industry runs on anonymous shells and hidden lab reports. Both are solvable problems.

How we operate

The anonymous LLC

Most vendors operate from offshore mailbox addresses behind companies with no named officers. When one shuts down, accounts, COA records and refund options vanish with it. VRC Solutions LLC is a registered US limited liability company with a public address and state tax accounts on file.

The hidden Certificate of Analysis

Plenty of sellers claim third-party testing, then ask you to email for the report, or hide it behind a login, or show one certificate from a batch years old. Ours are published before sale and linked from every product page. No email required. No account required.

Source, test, publish, ship

Every batch follows the same four steps, in the same order, with no shortcuts.

Source

Research-grade material sourced from suppliers with documented quality standards and traceable production records.

Test

A sample from every production batch goes to an independent third-party laboratory for HPLC and mass spectrometry verification.

Publish

The Certificate of Analysis is added to the public COA library and linked from the product page before any vial ships.

Ship

US-based fulfillment, discreet packaging, tracked next-business-day dispatch on orders placed before 2pm ET.

What researchers ask before buying

How do I verify what I am buying?
Every batch's independent third-party COA is published and linked directly from the product page. You can check purity and identity before you place an order, without contacting us.
What is the refund policy?
Shipments confirmed lost in transit or that arrive damaged are refunded. We do not refund based on whether a product produced a particular research result.
Why don't you sell GLP-1s, SARMs or syringes?
Each category adds disproportionate compliance risk for customers and for the business. Keeping the catalog tight is a deliberate choice, not a supply limitation.
Do you ship internationally?
No. We ship within the United States only.
