It is the buyer's responsibility to confirm that purchase, possession and intended research use are lawful in their state and locality, and to handle all material in accordance with standard laboratory safety practice. Conditions of use after delivery are outside our control.

We reserve the right to refuse or cancel any order, at any time, where we believe the stated or implied purpose falls outside this policy. Refused orders are refunded in full.
