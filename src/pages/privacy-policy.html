Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 26, 2026
Last Updated: June 26, 2026

VRC Solutions LLC (“VRC”, “we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, with whom we share it, and the rights you have over it.

By accessing vrcsolutions.co (the “Site”) or placing an order, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly to us:

  • Name (legal name as required for shipping and identity verification)
  • Date of birth (for age-verification purposes only; not stored long-term)
  • Email address
  • Shipping address
  • Billing address
  • Phone number
  • Account password (stored in hashed form only)
  • Payment card information (collected and processed by our payment processor; we do not store full card numbers)
  • Order history
  • Communications you send to us, including support inquiries and customer reviews

Information collected automatically:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device identifier and operating system
  • Pages viewed, time spent on each page, and navigation patterns
  • Referring URL
  • Date and time of access
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 8)

Information from third parties:

  • Payment confirmation from our payment processor
  • Shipping status updates from carriers
  • Fraud risk signals from third-party fraud-prevention services

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To process and ship your orders
  • To verify your age and eligibility under our Research Use Only Policy
  • To communicate with you about your orders, account, and customer service inquiries
  • To send you transactional emails (order confirmations, shipping notifications, account changes)
  • To detect, prevent, and respond to fraud and abuse
  • To comply with legal obligations including tax reporting and law-enforcement requests
  • To enforce our Terms of Service and Research Use Only Policy
  • To improve our Site and Services
  • To send you marketing communications about our products (only if you have opted in, and you may opt out at any time)

3. Legal Bases for Processing

  • Performance of a contract: to process and ship your orders and provide customer service
  • Compliance with legal obligations: to meet tax, record-keeping, and law-enforcement requirements
  • Legitimate interests: to operate our business, prevent fraud, secure our systems, and improve our Site
  • Consent: to send marketing communications, where required by law

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the following categories of recipients:

  • Payment processors: to process your payment
  • Shipping carriers: USPS, UPS, FedEx, or others, to deliver your order
  • Fraud-prevention services: to verify your identity and detect fraudulent orders
  • Hosting and IT service providers: who maintain our Site infrastructure
  • Professional advisors: including our accountants and attorneys, when needed for business operations
  • Government authorities and law enforcement: when required by law, valid legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of VRC or others

We do not share your information with advertising networks, data brokers, or marketing partners.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as is necessary to:

  • Fulfill the purposes described in Section 2
  • Meet our legal, tax, and accounting obligations (typically 7 years for financial records)
  • Resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, and protect our legal rights

When personal information is no longer necessary, we delete it or anonymize it.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information including:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmitted to and from our Site
  • Password hashing (we never store plaintext passwords)
  • Restricted internal access on a need-to-know basis
  • Use of reputable third-party processors that maintain their own security standards

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your state of residence, you may have one or more of the following rights:

  • Right to know what personal information we have collected about you
  • Right to access a copy of your personal information
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information
  • Right to delete your personal information (subject to legal exceptions)
  • Right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell or share for behavioral advertising)
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights
  • Right to appeal a denial of a privacy request

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@vrcsolutions.co. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law (typically 45 days). We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Authenticate your session (essential cookies)
  • Remember your preferences and account state
  • Analyze Site traffic via privacy-friendly analytics (Plausible Analytics, which does not use third-party cookies)
  • Prevent fraud and improve security

We do not use Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, Facebook Pixel, or other third-party tracking technologies that share data with advertising networks.

You may disable cookies in your browser settings; however, essential cookies are required for the Site to function (e.g., logging in, completing checkout).

9. Children’s Privacy

The Site is not intended for and may not be used by anyone under 21. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 21. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a person under 21, we will delete it. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us at privacy@vrcsolutions.co.

10. International Visitors

The Site is operated from the United States and is intended for U.S. users. We do not ship outside the United States. If you access the Site from outside the United States, you acknowledge that your personal information will be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States and is subject to U.S. law.

11. Third-Party Links

The Site may contain links to third-party websites (e.g., our payment processors, third-party testing laboratories for COA verification). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any third-party site you visit.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last Updated” date at the top reflects the most recent change. Continued use of the Site after an update constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
