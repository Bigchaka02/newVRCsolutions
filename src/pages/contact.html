Response times

When you’ll hear back

We aim to respond to every email within one business day. We do not operate weekends or major US holidays. If you write on a Friday evening, you’ll typically hear back Monday.

For urgent shipping or order issues, mark your subject line with URGENT - Order #[your order number] and we’ll prioritize.

For legal or compliance correspondence, please include any relevant order numbers, dates, and a clear statement of the issue.
