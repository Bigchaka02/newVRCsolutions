Built on verifiable quality

VRC exists because the research compound industry runs on anonymous shells, hidden lab reports and quiet efficacy claims. We built the opposite of that, and published the evidence.

Two patterns we wanted to break

The anonymous LLC

Most peptide vendors operate from offshore mailbox addresses, behind companies with no named officers and operations hidden from customers. If something goes wrong with a product there is nobody to reach. If they shut down — and many do, frequently — accounts, COA records and refund options disappear with them.

VRC operates as VRC Solutions LLC, a registered limited liability company. The address is public. Tax and banking registrations are on file with the state. Support channels are documented and consistent.

The hidden Certificate of Analysis

Many vendors claim third-party testing, then ask customers to email for the certificate, hide it behind an account login, or show a single report from one batch years old. Some publish certificates that do not match the product at all.

Every batch we sell is tested by an independent third-party laboratory. Every certificate is published in our public COA library and linked from the product page. Verifiable before purchase, with no email and no account.

Our principles, plainly stated

Two lists. Both are operating policy, not marketing copy.

What we do

  • Sell only laboratory research compounds and reference materials
  • Third-party test every batch before it enters inventory
  • Publish every certificate in our public library
  • Operate under a named US LLC with a public Kentucky address
  • Hold strict Research Use Only discipline in every communication
  • Ship next business day, tracked, in discreet packaging
  • Refund orders that arrive damaged or are lost in transit
  • Treat compliance as a feature rather than an inconvenience

What we don't do

  • Sell semaglutide or tirzepatide (Novo Nordisk and Eli Lilly IP)
  • Sell SARMs, hCG, or controlled-substance-adjacent compounds
  • Provide dosing protocols, reconstitution steps or human-use guidance
  • Make therapeutic, efficacy or health claims of any kind
  • Refund based on research outcomes
  • Ship outside the United States
  • Operate behind an anonymous LLC or offshore shell

Source, test, publish, ship

Four steps, same order, every batch.

Source

Research-grade material from suppliers with documented quality standards and traceable production records.

Test

HPLC and mass spectrometry verification at an independent third-party laboratory, on every production batch.

Publish

Certificate added to the public library and linked from the product page before any vial ships.

Ship

US fulfillment, discreet packaging, tracked next-business-day dispatch.

What we deliberately don't sell

The catalog is narrow on purpose. These categories raise regulatory exposure for customers and for us.

No SARMs

Selective androgen receptor modulators carry FDA enforcement risk distinct from peptides.

No hCG

Human chorionic gonadotropin is a prescription drug. We do not sell prescription compounds.

No syringes

Consumption accessories imply end use. We sell research compounds for in-vitro work.

No human-use marketing

No dosing protocols, no before-and-after photos, no testimonials. Research use only, without exception.
