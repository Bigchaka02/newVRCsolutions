What you’re looking at

A Certificate of Analysis is a third-party lab report.

A Certificate of Analysis (COA) is a lab report that documents the purity, identity, and contamination status of a research compound. It is the most concrete way to verify that what’s on the label is actually in the vial.

Every COA below was issued by our independent testing partner, a third-party laboratory that specializes in research compound verification. We do not perform our own testing. Every batch is sent to an independent third-party laboratory before we put it into inventory.

How to read a COA

What’s in a Lab-Verified COA.

Sample identification

Product name, strength, and lot number — matches the label on your vial.

Purity percentage

The proportion of the sample that is the named compound (e.g., 98.5%).

Identity confirmation

Confirms the compound is what it’s labeled as, often via mass spectrometry.

Contamination check

Tests for residual solvents, impurities, or degradation products.

Date of analysis

When the laboratory performed the test on the batch.

Lab details

The testing laboratory’s certification and contact details.
