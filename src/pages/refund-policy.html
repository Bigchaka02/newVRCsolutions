Refund Policy

Effective Date: June 26, 2026
Last Updated: June 26, 2026

This Refund Policy explains the limited circumstances under which VRC Solutions LLC (“VRC”, “we”, “us”) will issue a refund or replacement on an order placed through vrcsolutions.co.

By placing an order, you agree to this Refund Policy as a material term of your purchase.

1. Refunds We Do Issue

We refund or replace orders in the following two scenarios only:

A. Lost in Transit

If your order is confirmed lost by the carrier (USPS, UPS, FedEx, etc.), we will refund the full purchase price or, at your election, reship the order at no cost. To qualify:

  • The carrier’s official tracking must show the package has not been delivered within 15 business days of the ship date (longer for some rural addresses)
  • You must notify us within 30 days of the original ship date
  • The shipping address on file at the time of order must match the address provided to the carrier
  • You must have made reasonable efforts to locate the package (check with neighbors, building management, the carrier, etc.)

B. Damaged in Transit

If your order arrives visibly damaged — such as a broken vial, crushed packaging, or compromised seal — we will refund the affected items or, at your election, replace them at no cost. To qualify:

  • You must notify us within 5 business days of delivery
  • You must provide clear photographs of (a) the outer packaging as received and (b) the damaged contents
  • The damage must be consistent with shipping damage (not post-delivery handling or use)
  • You must retain the damaged item and packaging until the claim is resolved, in case the carrier requests inspection

2. Refunds We Do NOT Issue

We do not refund, replace, exchange, or accept returns for any of the following reasons:

  • Efficacy or research outcome: We do not refund based on whether a product produced any particular research result, observation, or measurement.
  • Change of mind: We do not accept returns or refunds for buyer’s remorse, changed circumstances, or change of research direction after the order has shipped.
  • Wrong product ordered: It is the buyer’s responsibility to verify the product, dosage strength, and quantity at checkout.
  • Wrong address: If the buyer provided an incorrect or incomplete shipping address and the package is undeliverable or delivered to the wrong location, we are not responsible.
  • Delivery confirmed but reported missing without evidence: If the carrier confirms delivery to the address on file and the package is reported missing, we will work with the buyer to investigate but are not obligated to refund or replace absent confirmed evidence of carrier error or theft.
  • Storage damage after delivery: Once a product is delivered, the buyer assumes responsibility for proper storage.
  • Used or opened products: Once a research compound vial is opened, used, or partially used, it is not eligible for refund or replacement.
  • Customs seizure or interception: Packages seized, intercepted, or destroyed by customs are not eligible for refund or replacement. This applies to international orders, and also if a buyer forwards or re-ships a domestic order to an international destination on their own.

3. How to Request a Refund or Replacement

  1. Email support@vrcsolutions.co within the applicable notification window
  2. Include: your order number, the name on the order, a clear description of the issue, photographs for damaged-in-transit claims, tracking number for lost-in-transit claims
  3. We will review your claim within 3 business days and respond with our decision

4. Form of Refund

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Refunds typically appear on your statement within 5-10 business days after we process the refund.

5. Chargebacks

Filing a chargeback with your card issuer instead of contacting us first is considered a violation of these Terms. A chargeback filed in bad faith may result in account termination and may be reported to fraud-prevention services.

If you have an issue with an order, please contact us first. We are committed to resolving legitimate issues quickly and fairly.

6. Order Cancellation Before Shipment

If you need to cancel an order, contact us at support@vrcsolutions.co as soon as possible. We will cancel and refund in full if it has not yet shipped. Once shipped, the rules above apply.

7. International Orders

We ship to select international destinations. For international orders, the lost-in-transit and damage protections in Section 1 apply only while the package is in transit within the United States. Once a package leaves the US or enters the destination country’s customs system, VRC Solutions is not responsible for customs seizure, interception, destruction, delay, or loss, and no refund or replacement will be issued in those cases. The buyer is responsible for any duties, taxes, or VAT assessed by their country. International orders are payable by cryptocurrency only.
