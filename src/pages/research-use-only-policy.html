Research Use Only Policy

This policy governs every product listed on this site and every order placed through it.

Scope

All products sold by VRC Solutions LLC are supplied exclusively for laboratory research use. They are not sold for human or animal consumption, clinical use, diagnostic procedures or veterinary application, and they have not been evaluated by the US Food and Drug Administration for any such use.

What this means in practice

By placing an order you confirm that you are at least 21 years of age, that you are acquiring the product for in-vitro laboratory research, scientific study or forensic analysis, and that you will not administer it to humans or animals.

We do not provide dosing guidance, reconstitution protocols, administration instructions or any other human-use framing — not on this site, not in customer communications, and not in response to direct questions. Requests for that information will be declined.

We make no therapeutic, efficacy or health claims about any product. Nothing on this site should be read as a representation that a compound produces any effect in a human or animal body.

Buyer responsibility

It is the buyer's responsibility to confirm that purchase, possession and intended research use are lawful in their state and locality, and to handle all material in accordance with standard laboratory safety practice. Conditions of use after delivery are outside our control.

Enforcement

We reserve the right to refuse or cancel any order, at any time, where we believe the stated or implied purpose falls outside this policy. Refused orders are refunded in full.
