Frequently asked questions

If something isn't answered here, email hello@vrcsolutions.co and we will reply within one business day.

About VRC

What does VRC sell?
Laboratory research compounds and reference materials, sold strictly for in-vitro research use. Every batch is independently tested by a third-party laboratory and the certificate is published on the product page.
Where is VRC based?
VRC Solutions LLC is registered as a US limited liability company. Our principal mailing address is 9462 Brownsboro Rd, PMB 379, Louisville, KY 40241.
Why does the catalog have so few products?
Deliberately. Selling fewer compounds lets us verify each one rigorously, keep quality consistent across orders, and avoid categories that create disproportionate compliance risk.
Do you sell GLP-1s like semaglutide or tirzepatide?
No. Eli Lilly and Novo Nordisk hold strong patents on GLP-1 compounds and have actively pursued legal action against sellers. We skip the category entirely.
Do you sell SARMs?
No. We do not sell SARMs, hCG, or any compound that could be confused with a controlled substance.

Quality and testing

How do I know what I'm buying is what you say it is?
Every batch is independently tested by a third-party laboratory. The certificate is published in our public COA library and linked from each product page, so you can verify purity and identity before ordering.
What is independent third-party testing?
We use an independent laboratory specialising in research compound purity verification. They test for identity, purity percentage and contamination. We send samples from every batch.
Can I see the certificate before I buy?
Yes. Every product page links directly to the certificate for its current batch, and the full COA library is public with no login required.
Do you test every batch or just the first one?
Every batch. There is no scenario in which we sell from a batch that has not been independently tested.

Orders and shipping

How long does an order take to ship?
Orders placed before 2pm ET ship the next business day after payment confirmation. You receive a tracking number by email once it ships.
How long does delivery take?
Typically 2–3 business days within the continental US. Remote areas may take longer.
Do you ship internationally?
No. We ship within the United States only.
Do you ship discreetly?
Yes. Packaging carries no brand name on the outside, and the return address is our mailing PMB.
What carriers do you use?
A tracked US carrier. We select carriers on compliance and inspection-rate considerations and do not list the specific carrier publicly.

Payment and refunds

What payment methods do you accept?

Venmo, Cash App and cryptocurrency (BTC, ETH, USDC). Card payment is enabled for select regions — contact us to check whether yours is covered.

The honest reason we don't lead with cards: most processors classify research compounds as high-risk and decline to underwrite them. Rather than route you through a processor that might freeze or reverse orders mid-transaction, we use the rails that are reliably available to us. We would prefer to offer cards everywhere and are working toward it.

Is my payment information secure?
We never handle or store card or bank details. Venmo and Cash App payments happen inside those apps, and crypto settles on-chain through our payment provider. None of that data touches our servers. Where card payment is available it is handled by a PCI-DSS compliant processor.
When am I charged?
At the time you place your order.
Can I get a refund?
We refund orders confirmed lost in transit or that arrive damaged. We do not refund based on research outcomes or change of mind. See our Refund Policy.
Why don't you refund based on whether a product worked?
Doing so would imply we sell products for non-research consumption, which we do not. Our products are sold strictly for in-vitro laboratory research, and conditions of use after delivery are outside our control.

Compliance and legal

Are these products legal to buy?
We sell only compounds that are legal to sell in the United States as laboratory research materials. It is the buyer's responsibility to verify that purchase, possession and intended research use are legal in their state and locality.
Are these FDA-approved?
No. Our products have not been evaluated by the FDA for human or animal use, are not intended to diagnose or treat any condition, and are sold strictly for in-vitro laboratory research.
What does "Research Use Only" mean?
The products are sold for use in laboratory research, scientific study or forensic analysis only. They are not sold for human or animal consumption, are not approved for any therapeutic purpose, and are not marketed for any human or animal use. See the full Research Use Only Policy.
Do I need an account to view products?
No. The catalog and the full COA library are open. You confirm your age and research-use intent once when you first arrive, and you can check out as a guest. An account is optional and only stores your order history.
Why do I have to confirm I'm 21 or over?
Our products are not intended for use by minors under any circumstances, and we maintain a defensive compliance posture appropriate to selling research compounds.
