About VRC

What does VRC sell?

Laboratory research compounds and reference materials, sold strictly for in-vitro research use only. Every batch is independently tested by an independent third-party laboratory, and Certificates of Analysis (COAs) are published in our public COA library and linked from the product page.

Where is VRC based?

VRC Solutions LLC is registered as a US LLC. Our principal mailing address is 9462 Brownsboro Rd, PMB 379, Louisville, KY 40241.

Why does the catalog have so few products?

We deliberately keep the catalog tight. Selling fewer compounds lets us verify each one rigorously, maintain consistent quality across orders, and avoid product categories that create disproportionate compliance risk.

Do you sell GLP-1s like semaglutide or tirzepatide?

No. Eli Lilly and Novo Nordisk hold strong patents on GLP-1 compounds and have actively pursued legal action against sellers. We’ve made a deliberate decision to skip this category.

Do you sell SARMs?

No. We do not sell SARMs, hCG, or any compound that could be confused with a controlled substance.

Quality and Testing

How do I know what I’m buying is what you say it is?

Every batch is independently tested by an independent third-party laboratory. The COA is published in our public COA library and linked from the product page, so you can verify purity and identity before you order.

What is independent third-party testing?

We use an independent third-party laboratory specializing in research compound purity verification. They test for identity, purity percentage, and contamination. We send samples from every batch.

Can I see the COA before I buy?

Yes. Product pages link directly to the COA for the current batch, and our public COA library lists every certificate we hold. If a certificate shows as pending, ask us and we will send it before you order.

Do you test every batch or just the first one?

Every batch. There is no scenario where we sell from a batch that has not been independently tested.

Orders and Shipping

How long does an order take to ship?

Orders placed before 2pm ET ship the next business day after payment is confirmed. You’ll receive a tracking number by email once your order ships.

How long does delivery take?

Orders typically deliver in 2–3 business days within the continental US. Remote areas may take longer.

Do you ship internationally?

No. We ship within the United States only.

Do you ship discreetly?

Yes. Our packaging does not display the brand name on the outside, and the return address is our mailing PMB.

What carriers do you use?

We ship with a tracked US carrier. We choose carriers based on compliance and inspection-rate considerations and do not list the specific carrier publicly.

How do I check on my order?

Use the order status page with the reference from your confirmation email and the email address you ordered with. No account needed.

Payment and Refunds

What payment methods do you accept?

Venmo, Cash App, and cryptocurrency (BTC, ETH, USDC). Card payment is enabled for select regions — contact us to check whether yours is covered.

The honest reason we don’t lead with cards: most card processors classify research compounds as a high-risk category and decline to underwrite them. Rather than route you through a processor that might freeze or reverse orders mid-transaction, we use the rails that are reliably available to us. We would prefer to offer cards everywhere and are working toward it.

After you pay, we confirm the payment against your order and email you a tracking number once it ships. Orders placed before 2pm ET ship the next business day. If anything about your payment looks wrong, email support@vrcsolutions.co before doing anything else and we will sort it out.

Is my payment information secure?

We never handle or store your card or bank details. Venmo and Cash App payments happen inside those apps, and crypto payments settle on-chain — none of that data touches our servers. Where card payment is available, it is handled by a PCI-DSS compliant processor and we do not store full card numbers.

When am I charged?

At the time you place your order.

Can I get a refund?

We refund orders that are confirmed lost in transit or arrive damaged in transit. We do not refund based on research outcomes, change of mind, or any other reason. See our Refund Policy for details.

Why don’t you refund based on whether a product worked?

To do so would imply we sell products for non-research consumption purposes, which we do not. Our products are sold strictly for in-vitro laboratory research, and the conditions of use after delivery are outside our control.

Compliance and Legal

Are these products legal to buy?

We sell only compounds that are legal to sell in the United States as laboratory research materials. It is the buyer’s responsibility to verify that purchase, possession, and intended research use is legal in the buyer’s state and locality.

Are these FDA-approved?

No. Our products have not been evaluated by the FDA for human or animal use, are not intended to diagnose or treat any condition, and are sold strictly for in-vitro laboratory research use only.

What does “Research Use Only” mean?

It means the products are sold for use in laboratory research, scientific study, or forensic analysis only. They are not sold for human or animal consumption, are not approved for any therapeutic purpose, and are not marketed for any human or animal use. See our full Research Use Only Policy.

Do I need an account to view products?

No. The catalog and the full COA library are open. You confirm your age and research-use intent when you first arrive, and you check out as a guest — no account required.

Why do I have to verify I’m 21+?

Because our products are not intended for use by minors under any circumstances, and we maintain a defensive compliance posture appropriate to selling research compounds.
